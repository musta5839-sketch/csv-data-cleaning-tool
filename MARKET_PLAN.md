# Freelance Market Plan

## Priority 1: Upwork
- Freelancer fee: 0%-15% per contract.
- Egypt: Direct to Local Bank supports EGP.
- Withdrawal fee: $0.99.
- Typical transfer: within 4 business days.
- Official: https://support.upwork.com/

## Priority 2: Khamsat
- Seller commission: 20%.
- $5 sale = $4 before other costs.
- Bank withdrawal minimum: $250.
- Official: https://khamsat.com/

## Priority 3: Mostaql
- Freelancer bank withdrawal available in Egypt.
- Bank withdrawal minimum: $250.
- PayPal withdrawal minimum: $25.
- Official: https://mostaql.com/

## Priority 4: Fiverr
- Seller receives 80% of order value.
- Payoneer withdrawal available subject to eligibility.
- Official: https://www.fiverr.com/
